# User Story

- Story ID: US-0004
- Title: Implement Email Verification for New Users
- Status: Ready
- Priority: Must
- Owner: Scrum Team
- Last Updated: 2026-11-06

## As a new user, I want to verify my email address after registration so that my account is secure

## Acceptance Criteria
- Given a new user, when they register and receive a verification email, then clicking the verification link activates their account
- Given a new user, when they attempt to log in before verifying their email, then they are informed that email verification is required

## Notes
Users can verify their email addresses to ensure account security
